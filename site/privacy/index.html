Information Collected

Buyer intake may collect name, email, business name, workflow context, bottlenecks, offer details, and delivery preferences. Hosted checkout is handled by Stripe or Polar.

How It Is Used

Information is used to deliver the Snapshot, Deal Room, or Sprint, support receipts and access, generate fulfillment packets, reconcile payment events, and maintain the internal AI Money Challenge ledger.

Data Handling

Payment card details stay inside Stripe or Polar. API keys, webhook secrets, and private tokens are not stored in the static sales page. Operational automation should keep only the data needed for fulfillment, audit, support, and accounting.
